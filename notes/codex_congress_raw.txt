**Short Verdict**

The real bet is **Lattice Deduction + recurrent tied depth**, with **Clifford/geometric-product mixing as a potentially better learned abstract transformer**. Automata theory is a good lens for compression and depth scheduling. Edge-of-stability is a training diagnostic. HRM’s H/L biology story is mostly not the center.

“Mix all five” is a cathedral if you treat them as co-equal magic ingredients. A principled version exists, but it has one spine:

\[
a_{t+1}=a_t \sqcap \Pi_A(f_\theta(a_t, p, m))
\]

where \(a_t\) is a lattice candidate state, \(\Pi_A\) enforces monotone narrowing, and \(f_\theta\) is a recurrent geometric-product cell.

**A. Deep Unification**

Deep couplings:

1. **Lattice + recurrence: real science.** LDT is the strongest primitive here. It makes the recurrent state interpretable as an abstract-domain element, then forces each outer step to descend the lattice. That is why it can search, abstain, and remain empirically sound rather than merely “reason in latent space” ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.08605)).

2. **Lattice + automata/semigroup: real.** A finite lattice update is a finite-state transition. Iterating a learned deduction operator generates a transformation semigroup. The automata-shortcut result says bounded-memory recurrent dynamics can often be represented by shallow/log-depth transformer computations ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10749)). Use this to distill or binary-lift deduction steps, not as branding.

3. **Clifford product + recurrent deduction cell: plausible and worth testing.** CliffordNet’s geometric product gives a dense bilinear mixer with scalar alignment plus antisymmetric structural terms, and the paper’s key empirical claim is that this can make FFNs redundant in vision ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06793)). As a parameterization of \(f_\theta\), this is meaningful.

Cosmetic or weak couplings:

1. **Clifford + lattice is not naturally sound.** A geometric product is bilinear, not monotone under candidate-set inclusion. It can generate very expressive evidence, but the lattice projection must be the authority. If the GP cell directly eliminates candidates without calibration, it will hallucinate false deductions.

2. **Edge-of-stability is not an architecture.** The paper frames generalization through optimizer attractor dimension and Hessian spectrum ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19740)). Useful for LR schedules and probes; not a reason the model reasons.

3. **HRM H/L hierarchy is not the core bet.** The original paper proposes two recurrent modules and one-step gradient/deep supervision ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.21734)). Given your reproduction notes, steal tied recurrent depth, outer refinement, and deep supervision. Do not fetishize the H/L split.

**1. Geometric Lattice Deduction Transformer, highest payoff**

Mechanism:

```python
# a_t: lattice candidates, shape [positions, values]
h = E_lattice(a_t) + E_problem(p) + E_modality(m)

for r in range(R):
    c = C_graph(h)  # row/col/box/maze/neighborhood constraint context
    u, v = W_u(h), W_v(c)

    D_s = u * roll_s(v)                         # scalar/coherence branch
    W_s = u * roll_s(v) - roll_s(u) * v         # antisymmetric wedge branch

    h = LN(h + P(concat_s(D_s, W_s)))

q = sigmoid(W_out(h))        # candidate survival probability
conflict = sigmoid(W_cls(h_cls))

a_next = a_t & (q >= theta_elim)
```

Projection: only meet/narrow: \(a_{t+1} \sqsubseteq a_t\). Branch/backtrack as in LDT. Train on-policy with LDT’s alpha target, asymmetric BCE, singleton CE, conflict BCE, and deep supervision over internal recurrent steps.

Why it could matter: LDT already proves the outer form is powerful. The question is whether GP mixing is a better local abstract-transformer approximator than attention+FFN, especially for pairwise exclusions, rotations of evidence, and constraint interactions. The wedge branch is naturally suited to “these two candidates cannot coexist” style structure.

Kill risk: GP improves parameter efficiency but worsens calibration. One false elimination is fatal. If false-elimination rate is not lower than baseline after threshold calibration, abandon this as a reasoning architecture.

Multimodality: feed modality tokens into \(E_\text{modality}(m)\), but keep the answer state as a lattice. For example, image evidence proposes candidate objects/relations; text proposes constraints; the lattice narrows answer candidates.

**2. Product-Lattice Multimodal Abstract Reasoner, bigger but riskier**

Mechanism: define a product abstract domain:

\[
A = A_\text{objects} \times A_\text{relations} \times A_\text{text} \times A_\text{answer}
\]

Each component is a candidate set. Clifford mixing computes dense cross-modal bilinear evidence: object-text, object-relation, relation-answer. Projection applies symbolic or learned monotone narrowing per domain.

Why it could matter: this is the only multimodal version that is not hand-wavy. The multivector story becomes real only if grades have semantics: scalar = viability, bivector = pairwise relation/exclusion, trivector = higher-order constraint evidence.

Kill risk: you need an alpha target or verifier. Without a domain where valid solution sets can be sampled or checked, this collapses into ordinary multimodal classification with decorative algebra.

Multimodality: native. Start with CLEVR-like visual reasoning, Raven/ARC-like grids, or diagrammatic math where object/relation/answer lattices are explicit.

**3. Semigroup-Lifted GLDT, compression play**

Mechanism: train the recurrent GLDT step \(F_1\). Then learn macro-steps:

\[
F_2 \approx \Pi(F_1 \circ F_1), \quad
F_4 \approx \Pi(F_2 \circ F_2), \quad F_8 \approx \Pi(F_4 \circ F_4)
\]

Inference applies binary-lifted deduction, giving \(O(\log T)\)-style refinement. Keep lattice projection after every macro-step.

Why it could matter: it turns automata shortcuts into an engineering object: learned deduction macro-rules. If it works, you get lower test-time search and perhaps learned “naked pairs/X-wing”-like closure without coding rules.

Kill risk: macro-step distillation may break soundness. Stochastic branching also makes the operator less cleanly compositional.

Multimodality: secondary. Use after architecture 1 works.
